# Escalation matrix

| Trigger | Queue | Response time |
|---|---|---|
| Refund denied and customer disputes the decision | Billing review | 2 business days |
| Expected refund date has passed | Billing review | 1 business day |
| Suspected account takeover or fraud review | Trust & Safety | 4 hours |
| Accessibility complaint (captions, audio description) | Accessibility | 1 business day |
| Legal or press request | Legal | Do not respond; forward only |
